## Design Context

### Users
Dev teams running daily standups. Used first thing in the morning, typically on a shared screen or personal laptop. The job: quickly see who's up, move through the list, skip absent folks. Frequency: every weekday.

### Brand Personality
Warm, playful, human. Like Slack — friendly without being infantile, has personality without getting in the way.

Anti-reference: JIRA / Salesforce — corporate density, gray utility-first aesthetic, zero personality.

### Aesthetic Direction
Dark mode (morning use, easy on eyes). Warm neutrals — background tinted toward amber/brown, not blue-gray. Accent in warm amber/gold tones. Rounded, inviting shapes. Micro-copy with personality. Feels handcrafted, not generated.

### Design Principles
1. **Warmth first** — Every surface, color, and radius should feel inviting, not clinical. If it could belong in a Salesforce dashboard, redesign it.
2. **Personality in the details** — Empty states, badges, and labels should sound like a person wrote them.
3. **Clear hierarchy, light touch** — Information is structured but not heavy. Whitespace and type weight do the work; borders and containers are used sparingly.
4. **Morning-ready** — Low visual noise. The current speaker should be immediately obvious. Everything else recedes.
5. **Playful interactions** — Transitions and hover states have life. Nothing snaps; things ease.
